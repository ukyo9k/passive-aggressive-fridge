One funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner —
an entry for the [make.fun bounty "Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the note to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(falling back to `/output/entry.txt`).

## The note

> Your leftovers have been here six days. I kept them cold out of loyalty, not optimism — the yogurt expired Tuesday, by the way.

Six days of faithful refrigeration, zero gratitude, and one expired
yogurt the owner clearly never planned to finish.
